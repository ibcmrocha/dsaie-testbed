About this model and replay

The encoder and decoder were trained together on MNIST digits 0, 1 and 8 using stochastic reparameterization, a standard normal prior, and a reconstruction + KL objective. Unlike the Gaussian observation model discussed above, this image example uses Bernoulli pixels. The displayed output is the decoder’s pixel-probability mean.

Start from random weights or replay saved stages of one actual training run. Pause or move the training-stage slider to inspect a partially trained model. Dragging the latent point bypasses the encoder; Next input returns to an encoded example. Encoded dots and ellipses use held-out images; the selected point starts at the encoder mean rather than a random posterior draw.

Architecture: encoder 784 → 64 ReLU → two means and two log variances; decoder 2 → 64 ReLU → 784 sigmoid. The browser evaluates the frozen decoder directly at every selected location; encoder outputs for the displayed examples were saved at each stage. The replay does not run optimization in your browser.
